# Principles of Economics 3e, Principles of Macroeconomics 3e, Principles of Microeconomics 3e, Principles of Macroeconomics for AP® Courses 2e, and Principles of Microeconomics for AP® Courses 2e

[![Open in GitHub Codespaces](https://github.com/codespaces/badge.svg)](https://codespaces.new/openstax/osbooks-principles-economics-bundle)

_Principles of Economics 3e, Principles of Macroeconomics 3e, Principles of Microeconomics 3e, Principles of Macroeconomics for AP® Courses 2e, and Principles of Microeconomics for AP® Courses 2e_ are textbooks published by [OpenStax](https://openstax.org/), a non profit organization that is part of [Rice University](https://www.rice.edu/).

To view these books online and view contributors, please visit:
- _Principles of Economics 3e_ [online](https://openstax.org/details/books/principles-economics-3e)
- _Principles of Macroeconomics 3e_ [online](https://openstax.org/details/books/principles-macroeconomics-3e)
- _Principles of Microeconomics 3e_ [online](https://openstax.org/details/books/principles-microeconomics-3e)
- _Principles of Macroeconomics for AP® Courses 2e_ [online](https://openstax.org/details/books/principles-macroeconomics-ap-courses-2e)
- _Principles of Microeconomics for AP® Courses 2e_ [online](https://openstax.org/details/books/principles-microeconomics-ap-courses-2e)
